# Business Visual Identity / Artworks

Status: Dockside Golden Reference pass in progress.

## Goal

Give each current Business a recognizable visual identity while preserving one coherent Solara City art language. The first approved asset is Dockside; all later Business art must derive from its camera, lighting, detail density and crop behavior rather than inventing a new style per card.

## Golden Reference order

1. Dockside Detail — establish the Golden Reference.
2. Neon Laundry — derive the same visual grammar with cleaner utility / laundromat cues.
3. Afterdark Customs — derive the same grammar with performance-shop / street-scene cues.
4. Solara Nights — derive the same grammar with premium nightlife cues.

No later Business artwork is promoted before Dockside is explicitly approved.

## Dockside candidate contract

The Dockside reference should read as a believable premium game-rendered business exterior inside Solara City:

- cinematic exterior three-quarter view;
- nighttime / late-evening coastal industrial district;
- wet asphalt and believable material response;
- controlled cyan/pink practical lighting with warm industrial highlights;
- dock containers, service-bay / detailing cues and port infrastructure supporting the location without dominating it;
- the establishment remains the focal point;
- no real-world brands or franchise typography;
- no baked gameplay text, prices, stats, requirements or UI labels;
- leave quiet margins so one source image survives desktop, tablet and narrow-card crops;
- detailed stylized realism consistent with the existing Kairo KX-R rendering direction rather than photoreal stock photography or cartoon art.

## Crop / responsive contract

The production artwork must remain useful at wide desktop and narrow stacked-card widths. Keep the main storefront and recognizable Dockside cues near the central safe zone; secondary props may be lost at narrow crops. Critical gameplay information remains HTML.

Artwork presentation must use fluid width, contained overflow and a stable aspect ratio. Asset paths stay presentation-only and never enter GameState / Save v17 / CE1.

## Approval gate

Generated candidates are review material only. A candidate becomes the Dockside Golden Reference only after explicit user visual approval. After approval, document the chosen composition and then integrate an optimized runtime asset plus a presentation registry mapping the stable Business ID to the artwork.

Only after the integrated Dockside reference passes live desktop/mobile review may the same direction extend to Neon Laundry, Afterdark Customs and Solara Nights.

## Freeze

This phase is visual-only. Business prices, production, levels, gates, upgrade formulas, automation, offline behavior, Save v17 / CE1, RNG, localization and runtime authority remain unchanged.
