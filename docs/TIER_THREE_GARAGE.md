# Garage V-A — Tier-3 Vehicle Progression & Catalog Design

Status: **analysis/design phase started** after Operations Balance II (Save v27 / CE1).

This phase turns the existing Tier-3 wishlist into an executable progression proposal before any new vehicle, artwork, save migration, price, gate, or modifier is shipped.

## Baseline

Current live garage: six vehicles through Garage IV-D. Tier-2 customization and Workshop purchase insight are released. Operations Balance II is merged. The wider vehicle catalog remains planning-only beyond the six live vehicles.

## Tier-3 candidates

The existing catalog defines four Tier-3 identities:

| Vehicle | Internal archetype | Intended personality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Toseki Raizan | Lancer Evolution IX-era AWD icon | Grip / launch / Heat utility; major tuner milestone |
| Namera Luma | RX-7 FD-era lightweight sports coupe | Active specialist; lightweight performance and future racing depth |
| Toseki Tenrai | Supra Mk4-era tuner GT | High-output long-session / automation-oriented build |
| Sevrin Caron | Modern executive sport fastback | Passive-business anchor and patient long-session alternative |

Raizan is the leading candidate for the first Tier-3 production pilot, but V-A does **not** approve its artwork or implementation.

## V-A questions to resolve

1. Measure current progression after Operations Balance II using the real Business, vehicle, tuning, Crew, Heat, District, skill, Rebirth and automation rules.
2. Rebalance the historical Tier-3 price and gate proposals against the six live vehicles and their twelve Workshop setups.
3. Give every Tier-3 vehicle a distinct economic role without creating one universal best car.
4. Preserve the one-active-vehicle rule and replacement semantics for fitted setups.
5. Define opportunity costs and acquisition order so Tier-3 is a meaningful mid/late-game collection milestone rather than an immediate post-Tier-2 purchase.
6. Produce executable analysis cases for fresh acquisition boundaries, representative portfolios, active/manual/Dispatcher/passive play styles, Heat utility and Rebirth retention.
7. Recommend the bounded V-B pilot vehicle, price, gates and base effect. Artwork remains a separate explicit approval boundary.

## Constraints

- No real-world manufacturer/model names in player-facing copy.
- No copied logos or exact vehicle replicas; existing fictionalization rules remain authoritative.
- No new save schema unless the eventual implementation actually needs one.
- No free vehicle grants or migration-side unlocks.
- No unrelated economy rebalance.
- EN / DE / Villager remain release requirements for eventual implementation.
- Existing player progress must remain intact.

## Expected V-A output

V-A completes when the repository contains a numerically justified Tier-3 recommendation with executable analysis coverage and a clearly bounded V-B pilot specification. Implementation, artwork generation/integration, merge and deployment are separate checkpoints.
